No pressure

If you can't donate, you can still help more than you think:

  • Star the repos on GitHub.
  • Report bugs and suggest features.
  • Share Alpine.2K with other modders.
  • Write a guide or contribute translations.

Transparency

Every dollar goes straight back into the project. We don't sell user data, we don't run ads, and we don't lock content behind a paywall.
