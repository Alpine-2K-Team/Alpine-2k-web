How it works

  • Mod your console (CFW, HEN, RGH, JTAG, Homebrew Channel, etc.).
  • Open your console's Alpine.2K repo and download the latest release.
  • Install it with your homebrew loader.
  • Open Alpine.2K on your console and browse every other release for that system.
