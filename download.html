Get the app

Download Alpine.2K

Pick your modded console below. Install the Alpine.2K app once, then browse and download games and homebrew directly from your console.

How it works

Three steps

1. Mod your console

Make sure your console is modded (CFW, HEN, RGH, JTAG, Homebrew Channel, etc.).

2. Install Alpine.2K

Download the Alpine.2K build for your console and install it with your homebrew loader.

3. Open and download

Launch Alpine.2K on your console — browse games and homebrew, tap install, done.

Builds

Alpine.2K for your console

Requirements

  • A modded console you own
  • The homebrew loader for your console (Atmosphere, HEN, Aurora, Homebrew Channel, etc.)
  • Network connection on your console

Which file do I need?

Each console has its own Alpine.2K build. Pick the one that matches your console and mod method. If you're unsure, read the guide linked on each download card.

Safe to use?

Alpine.2K only lists and downloads content you legally own. It never distributes pirated games or ROMs. See our full disclaimer.
