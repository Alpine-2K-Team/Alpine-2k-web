About

Alpine.2K

Alpine.2K is a homebrew app for modded consoles. Install it once on your console and it becomes an app store — browse and download releases straight from the GitHub repo for that console.

Every console gets its own Alpine.2K repo. The app lives on your console; the library lives on GitHub; this website is the front door that ties it all together.

What we stand for

  • Free forever. No paywalls, no premium tiers.
  • Legal only. No pirated games, ROMs, or ISOs — ever.
  • Open source. MIT-licensed. Fork it, improve it, ship it.
  • Community first. Built by modders, for modders.

How it works

  • Each console has its own repo: Alpine-2K-Ps5, Alpine-2K-Ps4, Alpine-2K-Xbox360, and so on.
  • Releases published to those repos appear on this site within minutes.
  • The Alpine.2K app on your console reads the same repos and downloads directly.

Get involved

Report bugs, submit guides, request features, or just say hi. Everything's on GitHub.

One repo per console. One app for everything.
